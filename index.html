About

Design leadership at the frontier of what AI can do.

I'm Alex — a design leader with 17 years building products for audiences around the world, from native iOS and Android apps to AI agents, trading platforms and developer tools. I've led design organisations at News Corp, Expedia, Virtually Human Studio, Alpaca Markets and Loan Market Group — across global corporates and high-growth scale-ups, managing designers, design managers, 3D artists and front-end engineers across three continents.

Along the way I've shipped a company's first AI assistants to its entire user base, authored a 13-category agentic-AI pattern library, built AI research functions and internal AI workflows from scratch, and led design through regulation, compliance and 0→1 launches. I design AI that earns trust by showing its reasoning.
